# Learning with AI

---

## AI/LLM knows everything

1. At least, it knows better than the average (possibly better than 95% of) human beings.
2. The rest of 5% human beings are so expensive to hire, so AI is the best option.
3. You can ask **anything** and it will give you good answers.

---

4. It will give you the best answer sometimes to blow your mind away.
5. It will lie to you (hallucination), and you **should** detect if something is fishy; and keep asking.

---

## Avoiding Hallucinations

1. Use at least two LLMs and possibly more.
2. The goal is to understand, so use LLM to expand your mind, not to get the answer for your homework.
3. You should be able to sense if anything is wrong logically (just like we talk to a human).
3. For professional work, we should build the verification system (Ontology-based Palantir-like system using RAG).

---

## Being the 5%

1. We can accumulate our knowledge quickly using AI/LLMs.
2. Build anything to learn; without this process, we will forget whatever we thought (being fooled) we learned.
3. Keep solving problems, and identify patterns along the way.
4. When you can build the patterns in the specific problem domain, you will be regarded as the experts (5% that AI/LLM cannot replace).

---

## Learn with AI interactively

1. Using AI is not about asking questions and getting answers; it's about learning how to solve problems.
2. The traditional problem-solving still works: define-design-devlop-deploy.
3. You should have the system to facilitate this interactive process: "Obsidian" seems to be the clear winner in this game.
4. It is an interactive process just like learning from a human being; this was not possible before, but not anymore.

---

### Warning: The surest way to ruin your brain

- Just ask, copy the answer, submit homework.
- It's truly dangerous as it will brain human brain.
- Our brain will think that all I need is clicking the button, and it will remove its thinking capabilities.
- When we can use LM/AAI to activate our brain, it's a tragey.

---

Understanding Al risk is like understanding electricity: you don't avoid it, you learn how to handle it safely.
— Joel Salinas, Leadership in Change

Maybe we can add

- If we understand how to use AI(electricity) wisely, we will do many things that were impossible before.

---

## Learn with AI by building things

1. We can define what we want to build (make).
2. We start by making the simplest prototype by ourselves.
3. We start by making the specification of the goal/final products.

---

4. Then, we build (or ask LLM to build) on top of our specification or prototype.
5. Don't be ambitious, add little by little like, ver2, ver3, ver4 ...
6. We should have the system (any system with Git/KMS/notes or anything) to make it possible.
7. We should expand our understanding step by step with LLM/AI.
8. Make a detailed documentation (you can ask LLM to do that) for coming back and relearning.
